IEEE PAPER · ACADEMIC CONFERENCE, AMITY UNIVERSITY

Hybrid Deep Learning Based Breast Cancer Detection Using VGG16 and ResNet50V2 Fusion Model

Using the BreakHis histopathology image dataset, the study explored VGG16 and ResNet50V2 models and a fusion/stacking approach to combine their predictions. The goal: make complex model outputs more useful for image-based classification research.

The work was presented following an academic conference at Amity University and published as an IEEE research paper.
